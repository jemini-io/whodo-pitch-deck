**Closing the deal is HARD.**

**Lack of Trust:**

- Customers struggle to purchase when they don’t know if they can trust the provider. They hesitate when they can’t get a simple over-the-phone estimate. Customers often have to pay a diagnostic fee just to get an estimate.
- Providers: It’s hard to vet a customer when alget a text or a phone call.

**Efficiency**

- Estimates today require on-site visit and take time
- Losing money on every truck roll just for estimates
- Responding to emergencies takes 1 hr on average that’s hurts our biz
