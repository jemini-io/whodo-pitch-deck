# whodo — Competitive Landscape
Research date: November 20, 2026
Website: https://www.getwhodo.com

## Scope and methodology
whodo is described as a video-enabled professional network for mid-to-large home services companies, supporting lead qualification, provider referrals, customer referrals, and reviews. Its live website features were not independently verified in this research pass.

Inclusion: Home-services-oriented products with video consultations, referrals, or customer reviews. Direct, adjacent, and emerging competitors are included.

Agentic depth: Single actions = isolated tasks; Full workflows = connected multi-step automation; Autonomous loops = AI systems capable of independently executing and adapting recurring processes. These labels describe product capability, not necessarily autonomous AI. Funding and stages require additional verification.

## Strategic interpretation

### Closest competitive threats
1. LiveSwitch: Most direct competition in video-led qualification and remote estimates.
2. ServiceTitan: Largest strategic platform threat for mid-to-large contractors due to its operating-system position and marketing distribution.
3. NiceJob: Strong direct overlap in referrals and reviews.
4. Birdeye and Podium: Enterprise customer-engagement and reputation competitors.
5. Houzz Pro: Professional profiles, project collaboration and consumer discovery.

### Potential whitespace
The opportunity is to connect three traditionally separate workflows:
- Pre-sale video qualification
- Trusted provider-to-provider referrals
- Post-service reputation and customer referrals

The key hypothesis is that a persistent professional relationship network can outperform disconnected video, CRM, and review tools. This remains a hypothesis requiring customer and competitive validation.

### Defensibility questions
- Can whodo demonstrate measurable improvement in lead-to-booked-job conversion?
- Can provider referrals create repeatable network effects?
- Are referral relationships portable between companies or dependent on whodo?
- Can the product integrate with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro and Jobber?
- Does whodo produce proprietary relationship or qualification data that incumbents cannot easily replicate?

### Investment diligence gaps
- Verify all current funding rounds, valuations and ownership.
- Confirm current enterprise customer counts and revenue traction.
- Test competitor video-call, referral and review functionality hands-on.
- Confirm whether competitors support cross-company provider referrals.
- Obtain whodo customer conversion, referral and retention metrics.
- Evaluate the defensibility of network density within local service markets.

## Research limitations
This is a preliminary competitive inventory, not a completed investment-grade diligence report. The whodo website could not be independently validated in this pass. Some companies have only adjacent overlap, and current financing, company stages, AI autonomy, and traction remain unverified. Do not use these fields as confirmed investor-deck claims without further primary-source research.
