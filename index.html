The problem

Closing the deal is HARD.

Lack of trust

  • Customers struggle to purchase when they don’t know if they can trust the provider. They hesitate when they can’t get a simple over-the-phone estimate. Customers often have to pay a diagnostic fee just to get an estimate.
  • Providers: it’s hard to vet a customer when all they get is a text or a phone call.

Efficiency

  • Estimates today require an on-site visit and take time.
  • Losing money on every truck roll just for estimates.
  • Responding to emergencies takes 1 hr on average. That hurts the business.
  • We don’t know what we need before we get there, and often have to make second trips for parts.

Pre-sale video qualification that plugs into the CRM the shop already runs — text and browser, no app, no middleman.

  • Frame: four categories, one gap. WhoDo is not another CRM and not another lead marketplace.
  • Marketplaces (Thumbtack, Angi, Yelp): own discovery and the lead. Contractor rents access to the homeowner. No virtual-consult workflow. Easy dig: “Aren’t you just a better Thumbtack?” Answer: no — we never take the lead; we help the contractor close demand they already have.
  • Video tools (LiveSwitch is the real peer; HandiY/FrontDoor unverified): closest product overlap on remote estimates. Differentiator, said carefully: bidirectional ServiceTitan sync, no-download text/browser path, and we do not become another system of record. LiveSwitch’s documented CRM hook is Housecall Pro.
  • CRM / FSM (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber; Podium as inbox): they run the business. Scheduling, jobs, payments. Virtual consult is not native — HCP bolts on LiveSwitch. Thesis: sit on top, don’t rip-and-replace. Biggest strategic risk if asked: ServiceTitan builds or buys this layer into Marketing Pro / the OS.
  • Reputation / marketing (Birdeye, NiceJob): post-job reviews and referrals. Adjacent, not the same job. Do not put them on the slide.
  • Callout: converting only ServiceTitan’s ~10.8K active base is a ~$65M ARR wedge inside SAM.
  • Callout: LiveSwitch is the honest product competitor on video. Everyone else is a different job.
  • If pressed on defensibility: measurable lead-to-booked conversion, ST integration depth, and whether provider-referral network effects show up. Don’t claim network effects you haven’t proven.
